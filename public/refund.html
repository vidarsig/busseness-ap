Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Effective date: May 2026

Short version: 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. Cancel within 30 days of purchase for a full refund, no questions asked.

1. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with your Jobboks subscription, you can request a full refund within 30 days of your initial purchase. This applies to all paid plans (Pro and Business), both monthly and annual.

To request a refund, email us at hello@jobboks.com with your order details. We will process the refund within 5–10 business days.

2. Subscription Renewals

After the initial 30-day period, subscription renewals are non-refundable. However, you can cancel at any time and you will retain access to your paid plan until the end of the current billing period.

We send a reminder email before each annual renewal so you can cancel if you no longer need the service.

3. How to Cancel

  • Email hello@jobboks.com with your account email
  • Or cancel directly through the payment confirmation email you received from Paddle

Cancellation stops future charges immediately. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

4. Payment Processing

All payments and refunds are processed by Paddle, our Merchant of Record. Refunds typically appear on your statement within 5–10 business days depending on your bank or card provider.

5. Exceptions

Refunds may be declined in cases of suspected fraud or abuse of the refund policy (e.g. repeated purchase and refund cycles).

6. EU / EEA Consumer Rights

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the right to withdraw from a digital service contract within 14 days of purchase (the "cooling-off period") under EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU. Our 30-day guarantee exceeds this requirement.

7. Contact
